# Privacy Policy (Draft)

Effective date: 2026-04-16

This Privacy Policy describes how DocSync collects, uses, and protects personal data.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to DocSync services used by organizations and end users.

## 2. Data We Process

- Account identity data: name, email, account identifiers
- Authentication and security data: session metadata, login attempts, MFA state
- Collaboration data: documents, comments, versions, to-dos, workspace metadata
- Operational telemetry: audit events and service diagnostics

## 3. Purposes of Processing

- Provide and secure the service
- Authenticate users and enforce organization-level access controls
- Detect abuse, investigate incidents, and satisfy security obligations
- Maintain service reliability and support

## 4. Legal Basis (Template)

Depending on jurisdiction and customer contract, processing may rely on:

- Contract performance
- Legitimate interests in security and service operation
- Consent where explicitly required

## 5. Data Sharing

- No sale of personal data
- Subprocessors and infrastructure providers may process data under contractual restrictions
- Disclosure may occur where legally required

## 6. Retention

Retention schedules and deletion workflows are defined in ../security/data-retention-and-deletion.md.

## 7. Security Measures

- Access control and tenant scoping
- Session and authentication controls
- Audit logging and administrative traceability
- Production requirements for encryption in transit and secret management

## 8. Data Subject Requests

Requests (access, correction, deletion) are handled through customer admin or support process and tracked via incident/operations channels.

## 9. International Transfers

If data is transferred across regions, contractual safeguards must be established before production use.

## 10. Changes

We may update this policy. Material updates should be communicated to customers in advance when required.
